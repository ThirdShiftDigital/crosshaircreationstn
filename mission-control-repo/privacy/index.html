Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Crosshair Creations ("we," "us," or "our") provides drone photography, aerial media, and search & recovery services in Middle Tennessee. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a service, or submit a form on our website (including real estate, construction, business photography, or emergency animal recovery requests), we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Property or location details relevant to the service requested
  • Any additional details you choose to share about your request

If you book a service through Square, Square's own privacy practices govern the payment and scheduling information you provide directly to them.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry or service request
  • To schedule, coordinate, and carry out the service you requested
  • To send you updates about your request (for example, status updates on a recovery mission)
  • To improve our services and communicate with you about your project

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Text Messages (SMS)

If you provide your phone number, we may contact you by text message to coordinate scheduling, provide status updates, or respond to your request. By submitting your phone number, you consent to receive these text messages from Crosshair Creations.

  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us (typically only in direct response to a request you've submitted).
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • We do not share your mobile phone number with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our business, including Square (payments and scheduling), and providers that deliver email and text notifications on our behalf. These providers process your information only to the extent necessary to deliver these services, under their own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention

We retain the information you provide for as long as needed to deliver our services and maintain accurate business records, and delete or anonymize it when it's no longer needed.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we have about you, or opt out of text or email communications, at any time by contacting us using the information below.
